## 6. Rubric

Rubric version 1. Faithfulness is derived from claim verdicts: Derived, not scored by the judge, and based ONLY on the evidence the answerer was given (never on gold facts). The application splits the answer into numbered units (sentences, key changes, mappings); the judge must account for EVERY unit, either by assessing it through claims (supported / unsupported / unclear) or by declaring it non-factual. A unit is supported only if all its claims are. rate = supported units / factual units; the 1-5 value is 1 + 4 * rate, rounded. No factual units => not applicable (never a perfect score).

**answer_relevance**

- 5: Directly and fully addresses the question asked.
- 4: Addresses the question; minor irrelevant material.
- 3: Partly addresses it, or buries the answer in tangents.
- 2: Mostly off-topic; only a loose connection to the question.
- 1: Does not address the question.

**factual_correctness**

- 5: Every statement agrees with the gold facts and the evidence.
- 4: Agrees with the gold facts; one minor imprecision.
- 3: Mostly agrees; at least one notable imprecision or omission of a gold fact's detail.
- 2: Contradicts a gold fact or mixes up versions/controls in a significant way.
- 1: Largely contradicts the gold facts.

**completeness**

- 5: Covers every expected fact and constraint.
- 4: Covers nearly all expected facts; a minor one is missing.
- 3: Covers about half of the expected facts.
- 2: Covers only one or two expected facts.
- 1: Covers none of the expected facts (or wrongly abstains).

**citation_support**

- 5: Every cited item clearly supports the claim it is attached to.
- 4: Citations support the claims; one is weak or only partially relevant.
- 3: About half of the citations clearly support their claims.
- 2: Most citations do not support the claims they are attached to.
- 1: Citations do not support the claims at all.

## 7. Limitations

- A judge model can be wrong, inconsistent and biased toward its own style; it is calibrated, not trusted.
- Using the same model (or vendor family) as generator and judge does not give an independent evaluation.
- Deterministic validators remain authoritative for mappings, versions, conflicts, citation structure and abstention; the judge adds prose-level opinions only.
- Gold facts and retrieval labels are AI-curated and not human-reviewed.
- Small samples (6 or 19 questions) cannot support accuracy claims.
