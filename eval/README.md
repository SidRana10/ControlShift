# Evaluation artifacts

Everything here is **exploratory**. The retrieval benchmark uses 39 AI-labelled questions (chunk ranking only); the live-answer evaluation covers **six questions**; the LLM judge has **not** been validated against human reviewers, and **no independent human review was completed**. Relevance labels and source checks were written by an AI assistant (Claude) and are marked as such in each file. Nothing here establishes general accuracy, and no model-confidence figure exists. Failed provider attempts are kept on purpose as evidence of what was actually run.

## Retrieval benchmark (no LLM)

- [`report.md`](report.md) - methodology, dataset composition, label provenance and results for the four modes (`bm25`, `dense`, `hybrid`, `hybrid_rerank`), generated by `benchmark.py` from [`results.json`](results.json).
- [`questions.jsonl`](questions.jsonl) - the questions and relevance labels; [`questions_audit.md`](questions_audit.md) is the audit sheet for reviewing those labels (no human has yet).
- Scripts: `build_questions.py` (validates labels against SQLite), `benchmark.py`.

## Grounded-answer evaluation

- Offline, no model call: [`answer_report.md`](answer_report.md), [`answer_results.json`](answer_results.json), gold file [`answer_questions.jsonl`](answer_questions.jsonl) (scripts `build_answer_questions.py`, `answer_eval.py`).
- Live, six questions (Groq generator): raw records in [`live_runs/`](live_runs/); human-readable review in [`live_answer_review.md`](live_answer_review.md); [`b01_before_after.md`](b01_before_after.md) documents one question's failure and its two post-hoc repairs (not an unbiased benchmark).
- Live smoke checks: `live_verification*.json`, `groq_judge_smoke.json` (small connectivity/behaviour records, not results).

## LLM-judge experiments

- [`judge_report.md`](judge_report.md) (generated by `judge_eval.py`), [`judge_results_live.json`](judge_results_live.json) (runtime artifact of the last live judge run), [`judge_plan.json`](judge_plan.json), [`judge_calibration.json`](judge_calibration.json).
- The judge (Groq `openai/gpt-oss-120b`) scores are opinions, not ground truth, and were not checked against humans. The generator used in the live run was Groq `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` (see section 3 of that report and `judge_results_live.json`); the result-types table at the top of the report previously labelled it "Gemini" in error, which has been corrected to a provider-neutral description.

## Provider-failure records

[`failed_runs/`](failed_runs/) - sanitized records of real Gemini 503 and Mistral 429 failures; its [README](failed_runs/README.md) lists each one. They are not results and nothing reads them.
